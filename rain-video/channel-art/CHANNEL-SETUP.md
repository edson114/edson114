# Midnight Rain Room — channel setup

## 1. Create the channel (about 2 minutes)
1. On youtube.com, signed in to your Google account, click your profile picture → **Switch account** → **View all channels** → **Create a channel**.
   (On the phone app: profile picture → your name → **Add channel** / **Create a channel**.)
2. Name: **Midnight Rain Room**
3. Handle: **@MidnightRainRoom** — if it's taken, try @MidnightRainRoomSounds or @MidnightRain.Room
4. Creating it this way makes a *Brand Account*, so it stays separate from CyberTravel and you can manage both from one login.

## 2. Add the artwork (YouTube Studio → Customization → Branding)
- **Picture:** `profile_picture_800.jpg`
- **Banner image:** `banner_2560x1440.jpg` — the name and tagline sit inside YouTube's safe area, so they show on phones, computers and TVs.

## 3. Description (Customization → Basic info → Description)

🌧️ Welcome to Midnight Rain Room, your quiet place to rest, sleep and breathe.

Here you'll find long, peaceful soundscapes for deep sleep, meditation, study and relaxation: soft rain in pine forests, gentle storms outside cozy windows, calm nights in nature, and slow, calming visuals that are easy on the eyes.

✨ What you'll find here:
• 3–10 hour rain and nature sounds for sleeping
• Calm ambience for meditation, yoga and breathing
• Focus sounds for studying, reading and work
• No sudden noises, just steady, soothing sound

🌙 New relaxing videos every week. Subscribe and turn on notifications so your next night of good sleep is always ready.

Sleep well. 💤

## 4. Channel keywords (Settings → Channel → Basic info)
rain sounds, sleep sounds, meditation music, nature sounds, relaxing sounds, deep sleep, rain for sleeping, ambience, white noise, study sounds, insomnia, stress relief, calm, forest rain
