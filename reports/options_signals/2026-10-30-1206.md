## How this works

- Setup (trader-scanner / trader-entry): the first 5-minute close beyond the 9:30-9:45 range; above it is a call, below it a put. Evaluated from the latest close, what you would pay now.
- Exit (trader-exit): stop at the range midpoint on the stock, out by the flat-by time.
- Odds (trader-timemachine): 10,000 replays from the stock's own 5-minute bars at the same time of day over its last ~60 days, the option re-priced each bar with Black-Scholes at today's implied volatility. Volatility changes, earnings and news are not in those replays.
- Cost: you pay the ask and sell at the bid, so the spread counts against every trade.
- Size (trader-budget / trader-risk): your max loss per trade, position cap and cash buffer, in contracts.
- Gates: the repo's FOMC/CPI calendar and VIX threshold skip the day; earnings today skips the stock.
