## 3. The bond put as a trade (QQQ daily, 2000 onward)

Buy at the open of the session after a spike close, exit at the close N sessions later, one position at a time. "Random entry" is QQQ over the same holding period from every session in the same years; p is the chance that random entries match the spike entries' average (low = unlikely to be luck). Calls: 0.80-delta ~60 DTE, Black-Scholes at VIX x the backtest's IV multiple (actual VIX at exit), $0.05/share slippage per fill; returns are % of the premium paid.
