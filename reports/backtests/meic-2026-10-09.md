Not financial advice.

**Rules simulated:** 6 entries/day, 55-wide spreads, $1.00-$1.75 credit target per side, stop at 2.0x initial credit (a 1.0x net loss), each side managed independently.

_Hard skip-day gates applied (gap and VIX-spike gates are backtested for real; the scheduled-macro-event gate only fires for dates present in `Config.macro_event_dates`, currently populated for 2026 only)._
